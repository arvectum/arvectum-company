## 1. Review purpose

This review tests whether AC-101 captures the business that Arvectum is actually building without turning historical conversation, product repositories or future ambitions into unsupported financial or market facts.

The closure question is:

> Is the current business-model/value-proposition baseline sufficiently coherent, evidence-bounded, commercially meaningful and safe to become the Company reference for AC-102 financial tracing?

The review explicitly does **not** approve pricing, budget, investment, product readiness, external commercial claims, departments, Positions, delegation or AI authority.

The executive labels below are functional review lenses only. Except for legally existing capacities, they do not create executive Positions, appointments, departments, employment relationships or Organizational Authority.

## 2. Review lenses

The cross-review used the same full-management perspective set established during the founding review:

1. Owner / Founder — strategy, business focus, capital logic and anti-bureaucracy;
2. General Director — executive reality, external commitments and operational truthfulness;
3. Finance — revenue/cash/economic-mechanism discipline and no invented numbers;
4. Operations — real workflow, fulfilment logic and executable process boundaries;
5. Product & Portfolio — product roles, productization and no accidental portfolio decisions;
6. Technology & Architecture — Company/Product/OS boundary, portability and reusable capability logic;
7. Commercial — customer, buyer, user, payer and value-proposition clarity;
8. Legal & Compliance — authority, contract-role wording and no internal-governance substitution;
9. Security & Data — public-repository safety, local/controlled data processing and no secret/customer payload;
10. People & Organization — no fake Positions or org chart before M2;
11. Risk & Continuity — dependency, concentration, overclaim and unproven-economics risk.

## 3. Iterative review record

### Iteration 1 — Owner/strategy: one Company or a loose list of repositories?

**Primary lenses:** Owner, Product & Portfolio, Commercial.

**Material criticism:**

- the current portfolio contains seven mapped nodes plus Arvectum OS, so a naive baseline could describe seven unrelated businesses and fail to identify a coherent Company thesis;
- conversely, reducing Arvectum to “Tender Agent SaaS” would erase the supplier/contractor economic model and real productized client automation work;
- the business model must distinguish a core economic thesis from supporting productization and capability layers.

**Reconciliation incorporated in the artifact:**

- the baseline defines Arvectum as a **procurement-centered mixed operator/productization business** rather than as one SaaS product or a generic software holding;
- four layers are separated: procurement supplier/contractor, procurement SaaS/productization, adjacent productized automation/standalone software and internal capability compounding;
- Arvectum OS remains a shared platform dependency/capability foundation rather than being promoted into an ordinary revenue product;
- product existence remains distinct from portfolio priority or investment approval.

**Result:** strategic coherence achieved without collapsing materially different economic engines.

### Iteration 2 — Finance: value-capture mechanism is not proof of revenue

**Primary lenses:** Finance, Owner, Risk & Continuity.

**Material criticism:**

- prior conversations contain clear business-model intent, but AC-101 has no authoritative current revenue ledger, cash statement or full contract register;
- describing gross margin, project fees or SaaS as “current revenue” would be false precision;
- client-delivery product evidence does not prove that historical receipts were booked to ООО «Арвектум» after incorporation.

**Reconciliation incorporated in the artifact:**

- each engine distinguishes intended capture mechanism from observed financial proof;
- procurement margin is described as the intended mechanism, while actual revenue/margin/cash is deferred to AC-102;
- SaaS is an Owner-confirmed direction, not claimed recurring revenue;
- productized client delivery is recognized as a real operating/product pattern, while current LLC revenue attribution remains explicitly unverified;
- the artifact contains no invented price, margin, revenue, cash or cost number.

**Result:** Finance lens PASS. AC-101 is usable as a tracing map for AC-102 rather than as a substitute for financial evidence.

### Iteration 3 — Commercial: distinguish customer, user, supplier and payer

**Primary lenses:** Commercial, General Director, Product & Portfolio.

**Material criticism:**

- the procurement supplier/contractor model and Tender Agent software model use the word “customer” for different parties;
- a procurement buyer receiving goods is not the same customer/user as a supplier or tender company buying tender-office software;
- suppliers in the operator model are counterparties, not automatically software customers;
- the value proposition cannot use one generic marketing sentence to promise identical outcomes across procurement, parsers, creative testing and proxy software.

**Reconciliation incorporated in the artifact:**

- procurement demand-side buyers, suppliers/tender companies, automation clients, standalone-product users and the Company itself are separated in the beneficiary map;
- Layer 1 explains customer-side contract revenue versus source-side supplier relationships;
- Layer 2 separately identifies supplier/tender-company software users and notes the no-catalog/RFQ-first design-partner assumption;
- one Company-level value proposition is kept intentionally abstract, while segment/product-specific promises remain bounded;
- unsupported ROI, win-rate, time-saving and market-superiority claims are explicitly prohibited without evidence.

**Result:** commercial semantics are clear enough for AC-103 to map the actual customer lifecycle later.

### Iteration 4 — Operations/Product: RFQ-first reality and procurement-scope conflict

**Primary lenses:** Operations, Product & Portfolio, Owner, Finance.

**Material criticism:**

- the procurement model would be materially wrong if it assumed a fixed owned catalog and known prices;
- prior Owner direction emphasizes `223-ФЗ + private industrial` procurement, while the current OS P6.02 validation contract is explicitly a bounded `44-ФЗ` pre-bid workflow;
- product validation scope must not silently redefine Company commercial strategy;
- Tender Agent/Tender App overlap and parser reuse remain unresolved portfolio issues.

**Reconciliation incorporated in the artifact:**

- the supplier/contractor chain is explicitly RFQ-first and includes supplier search, TKP collection, deterministic economics and human GO/NO-GO;
- the baseline records Russia, `223-ФЗ + private industrial`, goods and electrical-equipment strength as the Owner-confirmed starting commercial direction;
- current `44-ФЗ` product validation is acknowledged but explicitly prevented from becoming a Company commercial-focus change by implication;
- Tender Agent/Tender App and parser-family rationalization remain deferred to Phase 3 rather than being “solved” in AC-101.

**Result:** the baseline reflects the actual intended operating logic without creating product-boundary decisions.

### Iteration 5 — Legal, authority and security: automation must not become corporate authority

**Primary lenses:** Legal & Compliance, General Director, Security & Data, Technology & Architecture.

**Material criticism:**

- language such as “automated tender office” or “AI operator” can be misread as authority to submit bids, sign contracts, spend money or make final legal/commercial decisions;
- local-first and sovereignty language could be overread as a legal-compliance or security certification;
- the public Company repository cannot contain private customer contracts, banking, personal or operational payload merely to prove the business model.

**Reconciliation incorporated in the artifact:**

- the value proposition preserves human/company authority for consequential commitments;
- the procurement workflow stops short of implying that technical capability creates legal or Organizational Authority;
- local-first/controlled processing and technology sovereignty are recorded as evidence-bounded design differentiators, not compliance certifications;
- the artifact contains no customer-confidential data, personal identifiers, signatures, bank details, contract payloads or credentials;
- Company/Product/OS authority remains governed by AC-001/AC-002 and applicable legal/corporate authority.

**Result:** no material legal/authority/security overclaim remains.

### Iteration 6 — People, technology and risk: do not design the organization from the business-model document

**Primary lenses:** People & Organization, Technology & Architecture, Risk & Continuity, Owner.

**Material criticism:**

- a rich business-model artifact could tempt the Company to invent Sales, Procurement, Product, Finance, CTO or AI-agent Positions immediately;
- Arvectum OS/Data Platform could be misrepresented as cost-saving facts before economics exist;
- “productization from real work” may become a bias toward preserving every experiment instead of stopping weak initiatives.

**Reconciliation incorporated in the artifact:**

- AC-101 creates no Position, department, Assignment, delegation or headcount;
- internal platform leverage is classified as an economic hypothesis until later cost/workload evidence exists;
- every portfolio node remains subject to later investment and stop/continue criteria;
- productization is described as a path from real evidence to a reusable candidate, not an automatic promotion rule;
- owner workload, continuity and key-person concentration remain explicit later evidence tasks.

**Result:** the business model is informative enough for later organization design without pre-creating that organization.

## 5. Material issues and disposition

No material AC-101 issue remains unresolved.

The review carries forward these explicit non-blocking items:

1. **Financial truth is still missing.** AC-102 must determine actual LLC revenue, cash, recurring cost, working capital and obligations from authoritative sources.
2. **Active customer truth is still incomplete.** AC-103 must distinguish target segments from customers/clients that are actually active and paying.
3. **SaaS is a direction, not a current recurring-revenue claim.** Packaging and economics remain unapproved/unproven.
4. **Client-delivery revenue attribution must be verified.** Productized work predating or surrounding incorporation must not be assumed to be LLC revenue without financial evidence.
5. **`223-ФЗ/private industrial` vs `44-ФЗ` scopes are not contradictory when kept in their correct layers.** The former is current Owner-confirmed Company commercial focus; the latter is a bounded product/platform validation contour.
6. **Portfolio overlaps remain open.** Tender Agent/Tender App, parser reuse, Data Platform role and standalone product monetization belong to Phase 3/product governance.
7. **Internal platform leverage is not yet measured.** Arvectum OS value remains strategic/control/reuse evidence until cost/workload results quantify economic leverage.
8. **No organizational design is implied.** Functions/Positions/authority are intentionally deferred until after M1.
