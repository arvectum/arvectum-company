## 1. Review purpose

This review tests whether AC-108 turns the AC-107 hypothesis into an instrument that can produce real market evidence without silently turning planning assumptions into customer facts.

The review must prevent five failure modes:

1. screening candidates by generic “AI interest” instead of the actual organizational problem;
2. leading interviews toward Arvectum's preferred answer;
3. mistaking warm access or willingness to talk for demand/willingness to pay;
4. turning a design-partner concept into an unapproved commercial/production commitment;
5. collecting customer data or inferring Arvectum OS/customer authority beyond approved boundaries.

## 3. Iteration 1 — “Interested in AI” must not qualify a design partner

**Primary lenses:** Sales qualification, Commercial, Owner, Evidence.

**Criticism:** A candidate may be easy to contact and enthusiastic about AI while lacking a recurrent material workflow problem. If AC-108 treated interest, company size or industry as primary fit, the first design partner could be selected for convenience rather than evidence.

**Reconciliation:** The artifact introduces ten hard gates before prioritization:

- executive sponsor;
- bounded recurring function;
- observable accepted output;
- material management bottleneck;
- lawful/safe evidence access;
- explicit authority map;
- willingness to run supervised proof;
- viable fallback;
- willingness to baseline/review outcomes;
- no unsupported commitment requirement.

A numeric priority score cannot override a failed hard gate.

**Result:** PASS after correction.

## 4. Iteration 2 — A scorecard can manufacture false precision

**Primary lenses:** Evidence, Finance, Operations.

**Criticism:** Candidate scoring is useful for ordering effort but can appear more scientific than the available evidence. A single total score could also hide a fatal authority/data/boundedness problem.

**Reconciliation:** AC-108 labels the `0–3` score explicitly as a reversible discovery-prioritization heuristic, not a predictive/statistical model.

The score is limited to eight practical dimensions and operates only after hard gates. The artifact also states that high totals cannot compensate for a structural failure in authority/data safety, boundedness or observability.

Priority bands are operational aids and must be recalibrated if early evidence shows that they do not distinguish useful candidates.

**Result:** PASS.

## 5. Iteration 3 — Warm relationships and procurement evidence can bias the first segment

**Primary lenses:** Commercial, Product/Portfolio, Owner, Experiment Design.

**Criticism:** Arvectum has strong current procurement/tender evidence and several trust-bearing client relationships. Those are efficient discovery sources but can make familiar domains look like stronger markets merely because evidence is easier to access.

**Reconciliation:** The sourcing plan:

- prefers warm trust-bearing relationships for information quality, not as demand evidence;
- keeps procurement/tender companies as one strong subsegment rather than Company identity;
- explicitly includes digital-service/automation and data-heavy operations candidates;
- requires meaningful evidence from at least one alternative subsegment before one familiar subsegment is treated as leading;
- treats cold/referral sourcing as a tool for coverage rather than a volume target.

**Result:** PASS.

## 6. Iteration 4 — Interviews must reconstruct reality before presenting the solution

**Primary lenses:** Customer Discovery, Operations, Evidence, Customer Success.

**Criticism:** Asking directly whether a customer wants an “AI-native company” will generate politeness, novelty interest and solution bias. It will not test `ICP-H1`, `JTBD-H1` or `WEDGE-H1` reliably.

**Reconciliation:** The discovery protocol now starts with the most recent concrete workflow instance and reconstructs:

- trigger;
- accepted output;
- real steps/handoffs;
- tools/systems;
- senior interventions;
- exceptions, delay and rework;
- quality/acceptance;
- authority/data boundary;
- economic consequence;
- prior alternatives.

Only after current-state discovery does the interviewer present the Arvectum concept and ask what fits, what is wrong and how the customer would describe the paid outcome.

The Russian-first script avoids asking for desired percentage improvements or “would you buy?” as primary evidence.

**Result:** PASS.

## 7. Iteration 5 — A conversation count must not become fake statistical validation

**Primary lenses:** Experiment Design, Owner, Commercial.

**Criticism:** AC-108 needs a bounded first loop but an arbitrary interview number can be misrepresented as representative market proof. Conversely, an entirely unbounded plan can consume Owner time indefinitely.

**Reconciliation:** The plan sets:

- working candidate pool: up to 20;
- first-loop target: 8–12 completed **high-information** conversations;
- no claim of statistical representativeness;
- explicit replacement of unusable superficial conversations rather than inflating the count;
- at least two materially different subsegments tested;
- three independent high-information candidates with substantially the same problem shape as a minimum *pattern signal*, not proof of PMF.

Failure to source enough qualified conversations is classified as `insufficient evidence`, not automatic rejection of the market.

**Result:** PASS.

## 8. Iteration 6 — Customer value, buyer intent and Arvectum economics must remain separate

**Primary lenses:** Finance, Commercial, CEO/General Director, Owner.

**Criticism:** A customer may have a real painful workflow yet no budget/authority to change it. A buyer may express enthusiasm but not act. A pilot may create customer value while consuming so much Arvectum customization/support effort that the business is unattractive.

**Reconciliation:** AC-108 separates three evidence classes:

1. **problem/value evidence** — actual management load, delay, rework, capacity/quality/risk consequence and prior spend;
2. **commercial behavior evidence** — who owns budget, what must be proven, what alternatives are compared, and whether an authorized buyer takes a serious next step;
3. **Arvectum delivery economics** — estimated discovery, implementation, integration, runtime/support and Owner effort before any commercial commitment.

The interview script uses past spending and budget process rather than a premature “would you pay X?” question.

**Result:** PASS.

## 9. Iteration 7 — The first engagement must not silently become “free consulting until it works”

**Primary lenses:** Owner, Finance, Operations, Risk.

**Criticism:** Design-partner language can hide an unbounded consulting relationship. If discovery, diagnosis, implementation, correction, support and production commitments blur together, the Company cannot measure unit economics or stop cleanly.

**Reconciliation:** AC-108 defines two conceptual stages without approving either commercially:

- **Stage A — Diagnostic / function baseline:** map one function, accepted output, management bottleneck, authority/data/tool boundary, quality/economic evidence and fallback before production mutation;
- **Stage B — Bounded supervised pilot:** only after separate approval/agreement, test one function with representative real cases, explicit human authority, approved access, fallback and before/after evidence.

Price, discount, support duration, SLA, liability, IP/data terms and production commitments remain separate Owner/customer decisions.

**Result:** PASS.

## 10. Iteration 8 — Negative evidence must be able to change or stop the flagship hypothesis

**Primary lenses:** Evidence, Product, Commercial, Owner.

**Criticism:** A market-validation plan that can only conclude “continue” is not validation. AC-107 already defined falsifiable hypotheses; AC-108 needs operational decision logic that can reject or narrow them.

**Reconciliation:** AC-108 defines three explicit outcomes:

- **Continue** when multiple independent candidates show the same material pattern, a bounded measurable function exists, authority/economics are credible and at least one authorized buyer demonstrates serious next-step behavior.
- **Change** when the pain exists but the buyer, main outcome, wedge, subsegment, sovereignty positioning or implementation scope differs materially from AC-107.
- **Stop/rethink** after a completed first loop when no coherent material problem, bounded function, fundable value pool or safe/economic proof path emerges.

The artifact also treats “customer wants a simple narrow tool instead” as valid evidence against `ICP-H2`, not as a failed sales call.

**Result:** PASS.

## 11. Iteration 9 — Customer authority, data and Arvectum OS maturity must remain bounded

**Primary lenses:** Security/Data, Arvectum OS, Technology, Risk, General Director.

**Criticism:** Discovery can easily request too much real data or credentials, and the flagship proposition can imply production-grade Arvectum OS capability or customer decision authority before those exist.

**Canonical re-check:** Arvectum OS `main` was re-checked at `d26f9583393d4f3d9ef104f5408439da0471fd76`. The Company-relevant baseline remains unchanged for this work: Constitution `1.2.0` and RFC-0001 through RFC-0008 remain binding within scope; the Company must not infer Stable Product Contract, Active capability, production readiness, SLA or customer Organizational Authority from current platform work.

**Reconciliation:** AC-108 now requires:

- minimum-data discovery;
- no secrets/credentials merely for discovery convenience;
- raw confidential interview/customer evidence outside the public repository by default;
- customer-specific evidence not becoming cross-customer knowledge automatically;
- explicit customer authority and veto mapping;
- separate approved scope before real system access;
- supervised/fail-closed/manual fallback for future proof;
- no Stable/Active/production/SLA Arvectum OS claim by implication;
- future OS reliance only through applicable Product Contract/governance paths.

**Result:** PASS.

## 13. Why the review closes at iteration 9 of 10

The Owner authorized a **maximum** of ten review iterations.

After iteration 9, remaining uncertainties are empirical by design:

- which subsegment will produce the strongest repeated problem evidence;
- whether the primary buyer hypothesis survives real conversations;
- what language customers use;
- how much management attention, cycle time, rework or capacity is actually at stake;
- whether serious buyers proceed from conversation to diagnostic/pilot behavior;
- actual willingness to pay and commercial structure;
- real data/integration/security burden;
- actual Arvectum implementation/support economics;
- whether sovereignty/replaceability changes purchase decisions;
- whether a first customer pattern transfers to later customers.

A tenth desk-review iteration cannot answer these without inventing market facts. The correct next evidence-producing action is to run the instrument under an Owner-approved priority, not to continue refining it abstractly.
