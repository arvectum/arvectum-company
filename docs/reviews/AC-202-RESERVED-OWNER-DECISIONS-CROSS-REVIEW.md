## 1. Review purpose

This review tests whether AC-202 creates the **smallest durable Owner-reserved decision boundary** needed for an owner-operated AI-native Company without either:

- allowing technical/organizational delegation to erode Owner control over fundamental strategy, capital, risk, authority and material commitments; or
- turning every consequential, ambiguous or currently Owner-dependent action into a permanent Owner bottleneck.

The review must preserve the sequence:

```text
function
→ decision authority
→ delegated Position authority
→ Assignment/executor
→ technical access
→ governed execution
```

and prevent authority from being inferred in the reverse direction.

## 3. Iteration 1 — separate Owner governance from participant and General Director legal capacity

**Primary lenses:** Owner, participant, General Director, governance.

**Criticism:** the current Owner is also the sole participant and General Director. A Reserved Owner Decision policy could therefore silently collapse three different capacities and later become legally wrong if those roles diverge.

**Reconciliation:** the proposal explicitly separates:

- internal Owner Organizational Authority;
- participant/general-meeting corporate competence;
- General Director / power-of-attorney / bank/signature execution;
- technical authorization.

The proposal treats legal/corporate competence as higher-authority and deliberately refuses to copy a supposedly exhaustive statutory list into Company policy. Where both an internal Reserved Owner Decision and a corporate/legal act are required, both gates apply.

The current legal/corporate baseline is used only to support the present one-participant/one-General-Director/standard-charter fact pattern; personal identifiers and sensitive payload are excluded.

**Result:** PASS after capacity separation.

## 4. Iteration 2 — do not reserve all material or difficult work to the Owner

**Primary lenses:** Owner workload, organizational design, operations, scalability.

**Criticism:** Company Constitution `3.5` currently preserves residual material authority with the Owner until delegation exists. If AC-202 simply restated “all material decisions are Owner-reserved”, AC-203 would have no meaningful delegation space and AC-104's Owner-bottleneck problem would become permanent governance.

**Reconciliation:** the proposal creates three explicit buckets:

- `A — Reserved Owner Decision` — permanently Owner-reserved absent amendment;
- `B — Delegable decision/action` — intended for Position authority envelopes;
- `C — Temporary residual Owner authority` — Owner-held only because no approved delegation exists yet or the case exceeds an envelope.

The proposal also states that intellectual difficulty, customer involvement, production impact, current Owner execution, privileged credentials or absence of a playbook do not by themselves justify permanent Owner reservation.

**Result:** PASS after residual-versus-reserved distinction.

## 5. Iteration 3 — strategy and capital must remain reserved without inventing a budget regime

**Primary lenses:** Strategy, Finance, Owner, evidence.

**Criticism:** the policy must protect mission, flagship direction, capital allocation and material financial exposure, but the Company does not yet have evidence for universal ruble thresholds, annual department budgets or mature finance governance.

**Reconciliation:** `ROD-01` reserves mission/strategic/business-model identity changes, while `ROD-02` reserves capital-allocation principles, material financial exposures outside future approved envelopes and material economics exceptions.

The proposal explicitly refuses to invent numeric thresholds and sends practical limits to AC-203/later finance governance. Reporting preparation, accounting/tax execution and bounded spend inside a future approved envelope are not Owner-reserved merely because they involve money.

**Result:** PASS.

## 6. Iteration 4 — commercial and portfolio reservations must protect commitments without making the Owner the universal sales/product approver

**Primary lenses:** Commercial, Customer Delivery, Portfolio, Owner workload.

**Criticism:** current scoping, customer commitment and productization are heavily Owner-dependent. Reserving every proposal, price, release, customer message, scope decision or portfolio priority would preserve exactly the bottleneck AC-104 identified.

**Reconciliation:** the proposal reserves **exception/consequence boundaries**, not whole commercial/product functions:

- `ROD-03` covers material external commitments and non-standard commercial exposure outside an approved envelope;
- `ROD-04` covers major portfolio/initiative start-stop-redirection and material classification/investment changes.

Discovery, qualification, proposal preparation, routine customer communication, committed-scope delivery, product backlog sequencing, technical release planning and reuse-candidate preparation remain delegable in principle.

The first flagship design-partner/pilot decision is reserved only when it creates material production, privileged-access, support, liability or similar exposure outside an existing approved envelope. AC-108 non-binding discovery remains unaffected.

**Result:** PASS after commitment-versus-preparation separation.

## 7. Iteration 5 — authority architecture must protect itself without turning routine governance mechanics into Owner work

**Primary lenses:** Organizational Design, Governance, Owner, AI execution.

**Criticism:** if AC-203 can later delegate authority freely, it could indirectly delegate the Reserved Owner Decisions themselves. Conversely, if every roadmap publication or Assignment change needs Owner approval, governance becomes clerical bottleneck.

**Reconciliation:** `ROD-05` reserves:

- Company Constitution change;
- this Reserved Owner Decision policy;
- material authority/delegation architecture;
- material delegation grant/broadening/revocation;
- material organizational-model changes affecting authority/accountability;
- any mechanism that would allow another Principal/Position/AI/software system to make a currently reserved final decision.

At the same time, evidence collection, cross-review, publication mechanics after approval, low-risk workflow changes and routine Assignment changes that do not alter Position authority are explicitly non-reserved.

**Result:** PASS.

## 8. Iteration 6 — risk, data and sovereignty need Owner control but Owner approval cannot waive higher authority

**Primary lenses:** Security, Privacy, Customer Sovereignty, Risk, Technology Sovereignty.

**Criticism:** broad “Owner may accept risk” language can be dangerous if interpreted as power to waive law, customer rights, binding contracts, OS constitutional invariants or security controls that admit no exception.

**Reconciliation:** the proposal separates three reserved classes:

- `ROD-06` — risk appetite and material exception acceptance;
- `ROD-07` — material customer/data-sovereignty reuse/disclosure exceptions;
- `ROD-08` — core IP, critical dependency and technology-sovereignty exceptions.

A strict non-waiver rule states that Owner approval cannot legalize an unlawful act, manufacture customer consent/rights, weaken an OS constitutional invariant, override a binding Product Contract or create authority the Company lacks.

Routine least-privilege administration, required deletion/minimization, reversible dependency experiments and ordinary tool selection remain delegable under approved rules.

**Result:** PASS after higher-authority non-waiver rule.

## 9. Iteration 7 — AI/software must prepare and execute around the Owner gate, not become the gate

**Primary lenses:** AI/software, Owner workload, approval semantics, governed execution.

**Criticism:** an AI-native company can still fail organizationally if the model treats a recommendation, favorable score, workflow completion or technical execution as implicit Owner approval. The opposite failure is requiring the Owner to perform every post-decision publication/configuration step personally.

**Reconciliation:** the proposal defines a Reserved Owner Decision as valid only after an explicit attributable Owner act and rejects silence, AI recommendation, technical execution, workflow completion and repository access as approval.

It then deliberately makes Owner approval a **thin gate**: after explicit approval, accountable Positions/workflows may perform bounded publication, synchronization, implementation and other mechanics inside the approved scope.

Material fact changes, new external effects, missing legal authority, changed data/risk conditions or new cross-repository commitments force stop/re-approval rather than silent scope expansion.

**Result:** PASS.

## 10. Iteration 8 — emergency containment must work without delegating material risk acceptance

**Primary lenses:** Continuity, Security, Incident Response, Owner availability.

**Criticism:** if every security/continuity action is Owner-reserved, Owner unavailability can prevent the Company from revoking compromised access, stopping an automated external effect or entering a safe degraded mode. That would convert governance into a continuity defect.

**Reconciliation:** the proposal explicitly allows later pre-authorized reversible emergency containment such as revocation, isolation, safe shutdown, evidence preservation and approved degraded/recovery actions.

What remains reserved when material and outside an existing envelope is the **risk acceptance** decision: knowingly resuming with an unresolved consequential gap, accepting new material exposure, waiving an otherwise waivable Company control or materially changing risk appetite.

**Result:** PASS after containment-versus-risk-acceptance separation.

## 11. Iteration 9 — preserve Company/Product/OS/customer boundaries and test flagship transferability

**Primary lenses:** Company, Product, Arvectum OS, Customer Design, Strategy.

**Criticism:** a Company Owner-reserved catalog can drift into authority over product implementation, Arvectum OS governance or customer corporate decisions. It can also become a dangerous fixed template for the flagship `«ИИ-компания под ключ»` offer.

**Reconciliation:** `ROD-09` reserves the **Company side** of material Company↔Product↔OS responsibility and cross-repository commitments but explicitly states that it cannot approve an OS RFC/ADR/capability lifecycle change or product implementation decision belonging to another governance scope.

Customer reserved decisions remain customer-specific and must be re-derived from the customer's legal/corporate authority, business model, risk and obligations.

The reusable flagship asset is the method:

```text
value / obligations / risk / authority sources
→ consequential decision classes
→ legal/corporate reservations
→ smallest internal owner-reserved set
→ delegated Position envelopes
→ bounded human | AI | software execution
```

The review also traced every AC-201 function and confirmed that only specific material gates are reserved; no whole function is Owner-reserved.

**Result:** PASS.

## 13. Why the review closes at iteration 9 of 10

The Owner authorized a **maximum** of ten cross-review iterations.

After iteration 9, the remaining material questions cannot be answered by another AC-202 desk-review iteration without pre-empting later governance or inventing operating evidence:

- exact Position-level delegated decision classes;
- numeric budget/financial limits;
- commercial discount/commitment limits;
- data/classification access envelopes;
- risk/consequence tiers;
- which Positions should hold which authority;
- automatic-execution boundaries;
- tested escalation and emergency runbooks;
- whether real operating evidence shows some `ROD-*` class is too broad or too narrow;
- customer-specific reserved-decision catalogs for actual design partners.

Those questions belong to AC-203 through AC-208 and real operating evidence.

A tenth iteration would therefore either repeat the same conclusions or fabricate downstream decisions.

Stopping at iteration 9 is the evidence-disciplined result.
