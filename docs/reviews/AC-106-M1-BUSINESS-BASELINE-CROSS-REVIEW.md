## 1. Review purpose

This review tests whether AC-106 closes M1 at the correct evidence boundary and produces a business-first sequencing decision without manufacturing market validation, financial precision, organizational structure or platform maturity that the evidence does not support.

The review must prevent five failure modes:

1. treating a completed discovery instrument as validated demand;
2. allowing internal roadmap neatness to outrank current customer/revenue/legal/risk obligations;
3. postponing market evidence until an internally “finished” AI company exists;
4. reacting to every portfolio/repository activity as if it were a Company priority;
5. using M1 closure to imply customer commitment, delegation, product/module admission or Arvectum OS production maturity.

## 3. Iteration 1 — M1 closure must not mean “market validated”

**Primary lenses:** Strategy, Commercial, Evidence, Owner.

**Criticism:** AC-101 through AC-108 are unusually complete as planning artifacts, which creates a risk that milestone closure will be narrated as proof that customers want the flagship offer.

**Reconciliation:** AC-106 explicitly defines the M1 result as a `ready-to-run business + market-validation baseline`, not validated demand. The M1 milestone itself is `Business/economic reality and first market-validation plan captured`; it does not require product-market fit, willingness to pay, a design partner or a paid pilot.

AC-107 and AC-108 unknowns remain empirical and are preserved rather than converted into conclusions.

**Result:** PASS after boundary confirmation.

## 4. Iteration 2 — Current obligations must outrank roadmap convenience

**Primary lenses:** General Director, Finance, Operations, Customer Delivery, Risk.

**Criticism:** Advancing to Phase 2 could become an internal-program priority that competes incorrectly with time-sensitive real customer delivery, cash, legal/corporate, banking/accounting or procurement obligations.

**Reconciliation:** AC-106 introduces `P0 — Protect current obligations, cash and material risk` as a preemptive exception band. A material existing obligation, security/data incident or continuity failure may interrupt the default Company roadmap sequence.

P0 is explicitly exception-driven. Ordinary incoming requests do not automatically become P0 and cannot turn the Owner back into an undifferentiated queue.

**Result:** PASS.

## 5. Iteration 3 — Market discovery should not wait for a “finished” internal Company

**Primary lenses:** Commercial, Strategy, Owner, Experiment Design.

**Criticism:** A sequential interpretation of the roadmap could lead Arvectum to complete all of Phase 2 before obtaining any external evidence, increasing the risk of optimizing the reference model around Arvectum-specific assumptions.

**Reconciliation:** AC-106 makes the first AC-108 discovery loop an immediate bounded parallel evidence track while AC-201 starts the internal reference-model track.

This follows the roadmap principle `Market evidence early` and creates a two-way learning loop:

```text
internal reference evidence ↔ external market evidence
```

Neither track is allowed to claim the other has already validated it.

**Result:** PASS.

## 6. Iteration 4 — Discovery must not become a new Owner bottleneck or activity vanity metric

**Primary lenses:** Owner, Commercial, Operations, Evidence.

**Criticism:** “Run discovery now” can expand into mass outbound, superficial conversations and high context-switching cost for the Owner—the exact bottleneck AC-104 identified.

**Reconciliation:** AC-106 preserves AC-108's bounded experiment:

- candidate pool up to `20`;
- target `8–12` completed high-information conversations;
- at least two materially different subsegments;
- recent concrete workflow evidence before solution discussion;
- unusable superficial conversations do not count;
- no mass outbound target merely to show activity.

The priority is information gain, not lead volume or interview count.

**Result:** PASS.

## 7. Iteration 5 — Phase 2 must remain a minimal real operating-model exercise

**Primary lenses:** Organizational Design, Owner, Operations, Evidence.

**Criticism:** M1 provides enough material to tempt the Company into designing an elaborate future org chart, departments, AI Positions or delegation matrix immediately.

**Reconciliation:** AC-106 advances only to `AC-201 — Minimal real organizational/function model`.

AC-201 must derive functions from current value streams, obligations, bottlenecks and control needs before later tasks define Reserved Owner Decisions, delegated authority, Positions, Assignments, access or continuity. AC-106 creates none of those objects itself.

The default rule remains:

`business function → Position/authority later → Assignment/runtime later`

**Result:** PASS.

## 8. Iteration 6 — Product and OS activity must not win priority by technical momentum

**Primary lenses:** Product/Portfolio, Technology, Arvectum OS, Finance, Strategy.

**Criticism:** Arvectum has several active repositories and substantial Arvectum OS work. Technical progress can create a false sense that the busiest repository is the highest Company priority.

**Reconciliation:** AC-106 separates:

- `P2` product/OS work tied to a real customer/revenue obligation, material continuity/security need or evidence required by the P1 loops; and
- `P3` speculative productization/module/platform expansion without current obligation or evidence.

Repository existence, commit velocity and architectural elegance create no investment claim by themselves. Phase 3 remains responsible for portfolio/module investment governance.

**Result:** PASS.

## 9. Iteration 7 — Missing cash/continuity detail is not a blanket M1 blocker

**Primary lenses:** Finance, Risk, General Director, Operations.

**Criticism:** AC-105 records unresolved credential, restore, data-recovery and representation gaps; AC-102 intentionally excludes transaction-level accounting. One interpretation could be that M1 cannot close until all such details are solved.

**Reconciliation:** The review distinguishes milestone progression from consequential commitment gates.

AC-105 explicitly states that its unresolved continuity gaps are inputs to Phase 2/4, not reasons to stop M1. AC-102 establishes a sufficient management-finance architecture without duplicating accounting.

However, any **specific material commitment** must still fail closed or pause when the decision-relevant cash, authority, rights/data, obligation or risk evidence required for that commitment is unavailable.

Thus:

```text
M1 progression may continue
≠
all future material actions are pre-approved
```

**Result:** PASS.

## 10. Iteration 8 — Discovery authorization must not become pilot/commercial authorization

**Primary lenses:** Owner, General Director, Commercial, Security/Data, Arvectum OS.

**Criticism:** Authorizing the AC-108 discovery loop could be read too broadly as permission to accept a design partner, collect privileged data, set price/discount, promise SLA/support, access production systems or run consequential customer automation.

**Reconciliation:** AC-106 uses the rule:

> **Aggressive in learning; conservative in commitment.**

The authorized parallel activity is bounded non-binding market discovery under AC-108. A diagnostic with material customer scope, a supervised pilot, price, discount, contract, SLA, production access, privileged credentials, external consequential action or Arvectum OS reliance beyond an existing approved boundary each requires its own applicable authority/agreement/governance path.

Customer Organizational Authority and customer sovereignty remain untouched.

**Result:** PASS.

## 11. Iteration 9 — Remaining M1 uncertainty is empirical, not a documentation defect

**Primary lenses:** Evidence, Commercial, Finance, Owner, Organizational Design.

**Criticism:** A tenth desk-review iteration could continue polishing assumptions about ICP, buyer, willingness to pay, implementation burden, Owner-time savings, continuity, first wedge and repeatability.

**Reconciliation:** The remaining questions require observations from real discovery, future diagnostics/pilots, prospective Owner-intervention measurement and continuity exercises. More abstract review would create false confidence rather than evidence.

The next evidence-producing actions are therefore:

- run the bounded AC-108 first discovery loop;
- start AC-201 and derive the minimal real Company function model;
- continue P0/P2 real obligation/revenue work when justified;
- preserve uncertainty until measured.

**Result:** PASS.

## 13. Why the review closes at iteration 9 of 10

The Owner authorized a **maximum** of ten cross-review iterations.

After iteration 9, the remaining material uncertainties are deliberately empirical:

- recurrence of the behavioral ICP across independent companies;
- actual buyer/budget/authority pattern;
- customer language and true JTBD shape;
- strongest first wedge;
- real managerial-attention/cycle/rework baseline;
- willingness to take a serious buying step and willingness to pay;
- real implementation/integration/support burden and Arvectum unit economics;
- value of sovereignty/replaceability in buying decisions;
- second/third-customer repeatability;
- measured reduction in Owner bottlenecks from Phase 2 changes;
- tested continuity/recovery behavior.

A tenth desk-review iteration cannot answer these without inventing evidence. Stopping at nine is therefore the evidence-disciplined outcome, not an incomplete review.
