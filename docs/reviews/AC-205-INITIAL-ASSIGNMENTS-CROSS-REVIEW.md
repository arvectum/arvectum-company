## 1. Review purpose

This review tests whether the Owner-directed initial allocation can become a practical Company Assignment baseline without:

- collapsing Owner, General Director and Position authority into one person-shaped authority source;
- pretending outsourced accounting is a Company Position or source of Organizational Authority;
- converting future human sellers into fictional current headcount;
- turning AI search/outreach into autonomous commercial commitment authority;
- making the AI-led Engineering Position dependent on Owner per-task approval;
- giving AI a Reserved Owner Decision merely because it performs most technical work;
- inferring credentials/access from an Assignment;
- or erasing the Finance/Security separation approved in AC-204.

Review perspectives include Owner, Company Executive, Commercial/Customer, Sales capacity, Portfolio/Product, Engineering/Release, Finance, outsourced accounting, Security/Risk/Continuity, authority/governance, continuity and flagship transferability.

## 2. Iteration 1 — Owner concentration must be represented, not hidden

**Criticism:** the proposed initial realization places the current Owner Principal in five of six Positions. A superficial AI-native model might hide this fact or create fictional independent agents to make the organization look more distributed than it is.

**Reconciliation:** AC-205 records the concentration explicitly as a transition state:

- Owner human Assignment: POS-001, POS-002, POS-003, POS-005, POS-006;
- AI-led: POS-004;
- external accounting interface: POS-005 plus bounded factual support to POS-002;
- future seller capacity: reserved, not active.

The model therefore captures current reality while preserving later reassignment boundaries.

**Result:** PASS.

## 3. Iteration 2 — the same human must act in distinguishable capacities

**Criticism:** because the same person is Owner and Position holder, any routine Position decision could accidentally be recorded as Owner-reserved and recreate the Owner bottleneck; conversely, a Reserved Owner Decision could be treated as ordinary Position authority.

**Reconciliation:** AC-205 requires capacity separation:

- routine non-reserved AM-0/AM-1/AM-2 work is performed in Position capacity;
- ROD decisions are Owner-capacity acts;
- participant/General Director acts remain legally/corporately sourced acts.

The proposal also states that the Owner may initiate a proposal directly; AI advice is optional input rather than a prerequisite for Owner action.

**Result:** PASS.

## 4. Iteration 3 — POS-002 accounting participation must not create a category error

**Criticism:** the Owner requested POS-002 as `я + бухгалтерия на аутсорсе + живые продавцы + ИИ-поиск и рассылки`. Outsourced accounting, however, is not commercial authority and AC-204 explicitly treats it as an external professional interface.

**Reconciliation:** the accounting provider is represented as a supporting `External-service interface`, not as the accountable POS-002 holder. It may supply invoice/payment/accounting/tax-status facts relevant to commercial work but cannot create customer scope, price, SLA or commitment merely because it provides those facts.

For POS-005 the same provider supplies authoritative statutory/accounting inputs while the Owner Position holder remains responsible for management interpretation and obligation control.

**Result:** PASS.

## 5. Iteration 4 — live sellers must be planned without fabricating current Positions/headcount

**Criticism:** the Owner asked to “закладывать позиции под живых продавцов”, but AC-204 currently approves only six Positions and no sellers have yet been hired/assigned. Creating named Sales Positions inside AC-205 would silently amend AC-204.

**Reconciliation:** AC-205 reserves `Conditional future human capacity` as additional scoped human Assignments under POS-002 after real hiring/engagement and an explicit Assignment record.

Initial seller work is expected to cover prospecting, approved outreach, qualification and follow-up inside bounded commercial rules. If live sales later becomes a separate durable accountability domain, AC-204 should be amended to create a dedicated Sales Position rather than allowing an Assignment to become a de facto new Position by habit.

**Result:** PASS.

## 6. Iteration 5 — AI search and mailings are useful but create an external-effect boundary

**Criticism:** AI prospect search is low-risk preparation, but outbound mailings are real external effects and can accidentally create promises, spam/compliance problems or customer/data-rights issues.

**Reconciliation:** AC-205 separates:

- search, research, enrichment, qualification and drafting — AI preparation;
- sending/follow-up — bounded execution only after campaign scope, recipient criteria, message class, frequency, suppression/opt-out handling and commitment boundary are explicitly approved and AC-206 access exists.

AI cannot invent price, SLA, warranty, scope or legal/data promises and cannot infer authority from an email/CRM/API credential.

**Result:** PASS.

## 7. Iteration 6 — AI-led Engineering must be genuinely AI-led

**Criticism:** calling POS-004 “AI-led” would be meaningless if every implementation/tool/testing decision still required Owner per-task approval.

**Reconciliation:** the proposal admits AI execution across POS-004's already-approved ceiling:

- AM-0 technical analysis/recommendation;
- AM-1 implementation/testing/packaging and approved release mechanics;
- AM-2 routine reversible engineering decisions inside accepted workstream, product, architecture, data, security and risk bounds.

Owner approval is not required for each bounded technical choice. Escalation occurs only when scope, acceptance, consequential external effect, risk, access or `ROD-*` boundaries are crossed.

**Result:** PASS.

## 8. Iteration 7 — AI-led does not mean AI authority source

**Criticism:** because POS-004 is predominantly executed by AI, the model could be misread as giving the model/provider independent Organizational Authority.

**Reconciliation:** the Position remains the durable authority/accountability unit; the AI model/agent/runtime is replaceable. No AM-3 or AM-4 is activated. AI cannot approve customer scope, capital allocation, material risk, portfolio investment or Reserved Owner Decisions.

This preserves the Company rule: AI is a means of execution, not the source of Organizational Authority.

**Result:** PASS.

## 9. Iteration 8 — Finance and Security must remain distinct despite one human holder

**Criticism:** AC-204 explicitly split Finance from Security/Risk/Continuity. Assigning the same Owner Principal to both could accidentally undo that separation in practice.

**Reconciliation:** AC-205 keeps POS-005 and POS-006 as separate Assignment capacities with separate evidence, handoffs and decision contexts. Commercial enthusiasm cannot become financial approval or risk acceptance by implication. Material decisions should record the capacity in which the human acted.

The same person may temporarily hold both without merging the Positions.

**Result:** PASS.

## 10. Iteration 9 — access and continuity must not be invented at Assignment stage

**Criticism:** the Assignment plan names AI outreach, AI engineering and outsourced accounting, but actual credentials, repositories, email/CRM, bank/accounting access, customer data and local-device paths are not yet fully mapped.

**Reconciliation:** the proposal explicitly treats Assignment as insufficient for execution. AC-206 must establish least-privilege technical/data/tool access, and AC-207 must test replacement/fallback.

Particularly important handoffs are:

- AI outreach is not operational until campaign/access boundaries exist;
- AI engineering is limited by actual repo/runtime access;
- outsourced accounting access stays in its legal/service contour;
- Owner credential possession does not enlarge another Assignment's authority;
- POS-004 must be replaceable across AI models/agents/runtimes without losing Position meaning or history.

**Result:** PASS.
