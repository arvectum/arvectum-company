## 3. Near-term priority decision

The Owner approves the following near-term priority bands.

### `P0 — Protect current obligations, cash and material risk`

Time-sensitive material customer, legal/corporate, banking/accounting, procurement, security/data or continuity obligations preempt internal roadmap convenience when actually triggered.

P0 is exception-driven. It does not make every incoming customer request, product issue or repository task a Company-level emergency.

### `P1 — Flagship market evidence + minimal reference operating model`

When no P0 exception is active, the default strategic priority is a paired evidence loop:

1. run the bounded AC-108 first discovery loop; and
2. execute Phase 2 beginning with `AC-201 — Minimal real organizational/function model`.

The external and internal tracks proceed in parallel so Arvectum neither designs the whole reference organization before speaking to the market nor stops improving its own real organization while waiting for market validation.

### `P2 — Product/OS work tied to real obligations, revenue or evidence`

Product or Arvectum OS work receives Company priority when it is required to satisfy a real customer/revenue obligation, close a material continuity/security problem, or produce evidence needed for the P1 loops.

Technical momentum, repository activity or architecture preference alone is insufficient.

### `P3 — Speculative productization/module/platform expansion`

Speculative module catalogs, broad platform expansion, invented AI headcount and other work justified mainly by future completeness remain below P0–P2 until evidence creates a stronger business case.

## 4. AC-108 discovery authorization

The Owner authorizes execution of the first AC-108 discovery loop as a **bounded non-binding market-evidence activity** under the existing AC-108 rules.

Operating rule:

> **Aggressive in learning; conservative in commitment.**

The authorized boundary is:

- working pool of up to `20` candidate organizations;
- target `8–12` completed high-information discovery conversations;
- meaningful evidence from at least two materially different subsegments before treating one familiar domain as leading;
- recent concrete workflow evidence before solution-led discussion;
- minimum-data handling and no unnecessary credentials/secrets;
- AC-108 hard gates, evidence schema and continue/change/stop logic remain applicable;
- raw confidential/customer-identifying evidence remains outside the public Company repository by default.

This authorization is for discovery/research only. It does not by itself authorize:

- a design-partner selection or commitment;
- a paid or unpaid diagnostic with material obligations;
- a supervised or production pilot;
- a price, discount, proposal, contract, SLA, support duration or liability term;
- privileged customer-system access or credential collection;
- autonomous external consequential actions;
- customer-data reuse outside the applicable customer scope;
- new Arvectum OS Product Contract reliance, Stable status, Active capability or production claim.

Any such step requires its own applicable customer agreement, Company authority, risk/data review and product/OS governance path.

## 5. M1 assumptions to carry as empirical evidence questions

The following are explicitly tracked rather than resolved through more desk analysis:

- whether the behavioral ICP recurs across independent companies;
- the real buyer/champion/budget/authority pattern;
- whether the JTBD is broad operating friction or usually a narrower software need;
- the strongest first bounded deployment wedge;
- measurable scarce-management attention, cycle, rework and quality baselines;
- willingness to take serious next steps and willingness to pay;
- real discovery/implementation/integration/runtime/support burden;
- Arvectum unit economics and Owner-attention cost;
- whether sovereignty/replaceability changes buying behavior;
- whether later customers require materially less reinvention;
- whether Phase 2 reduces internal Owner bottlenecks;
- whether continuity/recovery expectations pass real exercises.

Unknown evidence must remain unknown until observed.
