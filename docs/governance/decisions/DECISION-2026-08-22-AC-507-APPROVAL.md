## 3. Direction authorized

The Company SHALL continue `WF-M5-001` in a bounded evidence phase with changes aimed at improving empirical business/economic evidence rather than expanding infrastructure or authority.

Authorized direction includes:

1. preserve current CL-3/W11 fail-closed behavior and existing authority/data/customer/OS boundaries;
2. continue using linked-successor recovery only when genuinely new attributable evidence exists;
3. capture lightweight Owner intervention count/minutes on the next qualifying real case where practical;
4. capture coarse practical engineering effort avoided/incurred where applicable;
5. test whether evidence-handling ceremony, especially W1/W2 preparation, can be simplified without weakening control;
6. prefer stronger real-case evidence over additional governance/software layers;
7. keep the direction reversible and subject to later evidence-based review.

## 5. Evidence requirements carried forward

The bounded evidence phase should seek, without fabricating values:

- a real qualifying customer/workstream case that progresses farther than the current CL-3/W11 outcome where facts permit;
- measured Owner intervention burden where practical;
- engineering effort avoided/incurred where practical;
- customer-facing cycle/rework evidence;
- tool/runtime cost evidence when actually available;
- explicit customer validation/rework/acceptance evidence where reached;
- evidence sufficient to decide whether the workflow should remain bounded, be simplified, generalized or stopped.
