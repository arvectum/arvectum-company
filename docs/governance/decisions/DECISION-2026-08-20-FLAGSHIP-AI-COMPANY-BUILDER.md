## Decision

ООО «Арвектум» fixes the following Company-level strategic direction:

> The flagship commercial product direction of Arvectum is a configurable **AI-native company / «ИИ-компания под ключ»** offering. Arvectum designs and deploys an organization-specific operating model for a customer on top of Arvectum OS: business functions, Positions, authority boundaries, workflows, knowledge, controls and human/AI/software execution, adapted to the customer's actual business model and populated with reusable or customer-specific functional modules where justified.

Arvectum Company is both:

1. the real operating organization of ООО «Арвектум»; and
2. the first reference implementation / dogfooding environment for the organization-first model that the flagship offering is intended to deliver to customers.

## Consequences

1. Procurement is not the highest-level identity of Arvectum Company. It remains a real business/domain line, proof environment and potential procurement/tender module.
2. Arvectum OS remains the domain-neutral platform / Executable Organizational Model substrate. It is not itself the customer's corporate authority and does not own customer-specific business semantics merely because it executes or stores them.
3. Existing products such as Tender Agent, Creative Test Agent, parsers, Data Platform and Proxy Launcher may become standalone products, reference implementations, supporting capabilities or module candidates only through later evidence and portfolio/product decisions. This decision does not automatically classify or promote them as modules.
4. Customer organizations must be modeled from the customer's business model, obligations, authority and workflows. Arvectum does not sell a literal copy of its own departments, owner powers, history or data.
5. The commercial model should progressively become reproducible through a reference architecture, customer-discovery/configuration method, reusable module catalog, deployment method, support/continuity model and evidence-backed economics.
6. Internal Company development and external productization must remain coupled: Arvectum Company should prove the organizational method, while customer discovery and market evidence prevent internal architecture from becoming self-referential.

## Required roadmap reconciliation

The canonical Company roadmap must be updated so that:

- the flagship «ИИ-компания под ключ» direction is a visible North Star rather than an AC-101 footnote;
- M1 captures current economics and early customer/ICP evidence;
- M2 builds Arvectum Company as a reusable reference operating model, not merely an internal org chart;
- M3 governs products and module candidates with explicit reuse/admission boundaries;
- later phases prove internal governed execution and then a real external customer deployment;
- multi-customer repeatability, support economics, portability and scaling become explicit end-state evidence;
- no phase may infer customer/product readiness merely from internal technical success.

## Non-decisions

This decision does not approve:

- a specific ICP, industry or customer segment;
- pricing, packaging or SLA;
- any external customer commitment;
- a standard fixed department list;
- a specific module catalog;
- promotion of any current product into an Arvectum OS capability;
- an Arvectum OS Product Contract;
- budget or capital allocation;
- hiring, Position creation or delegation;
- production or legal/compliance readiness.

Those require their own evidence and applicable decision paths.
