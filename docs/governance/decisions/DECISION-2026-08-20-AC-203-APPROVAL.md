## Approved substance

The Owner approves the Company-wide delegated Position authority, approval and escalation model, including:

1. authority attaches to a Position boundary rather than to the current human, AI, software or runtime executor;
2. an Assignment may narrow Position authority but cannot broaden it;
3. five authority modes:
   - `AM-0 — Prepare / Recommend`;
   - `AM-1 — Execute Pre-Decided Work`;
   - `AM-2 — Bounded Decision`;
   - `AM-3 — Delegated Approval`;
   - `AM-4 — Pre-Authorized Automatic Execution`;
4. AC-202 `ROD-01` through `ROD-09` remain a hard negative boundary and cannot be silently delegated;
5. technical permission, credentials, system role, job title, workflow participation or AI capability do not create Organizational Authority;
6. effective executable scope is bounded by the intersection of Position authority, Assignment scope, technical authorization/access and current workflow/data/risk conditions;
7. missing, stale, ambiguous or exceeded authority produces escalation or fail-closed behavior rather than implied authority;
8. approval remains distinct from preparation, recommendation and technical execution;
9. AI/software are execution means and do not become independent sources of consequential approval authority by assignment alone;
10. emergency containment may be pre-authorized where reversible and bounded, while material risk acceptance remains reserved under AC-202;
11. concrete Positions, Assignments, numeric thresholds and access grants remain downstream AC-204 through AC-206 work.

## Authority and legal boundary

This decision approves an internal Company governance model only.

It does not:

- replace any legally required participant/general-meeting decision, General Director act, power of attorney, signature, bank authorization or other corporate/legal form;
- create any concrete Position or appoint a Principal;
- create an Assignment, technical permission, credential, access grant or bank authority;
- create customer authority, customer consent or cross-organization rights;
- establish numeric financial, SLA, duration, data-volume or risk thresholds;
- amend Arvectum OS governance, RFCs, ADRs, Product Contracts or capability lifecycle;
- transfer product implementation authority into the Company repository.

Applicable law, valid corporate authority, approved AC-202 Reserved Owner Decisions, customer authority and applicable Product/Arvectum OS governance remain controlling within their scope.
