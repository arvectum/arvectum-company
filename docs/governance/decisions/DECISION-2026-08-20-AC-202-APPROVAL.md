## Approved substance

The Owner approves the nine-class Reserved Owner Decision catalog and the governing distinction among:

1. `A — Reserved Owner Decision`;
2. `B — Delegable decision/action`;
3. `C — Temporary residual Owner authority` pending an approved delegation envelope.

The approval preserves the central rule that whole Company functions are not reserved by default. Reserved authority attaches to consequential decision classes and material exceptions, while bounded preparation, routine decisions and execution should move toward accountable Positions under AC-203.

## Authority and legal boundary

This decision approves an internal Company governance model only.

It does not:

- replace any legally required participant/general-meeting decision, General Director act, power of attorney, signature, bank authorization or other corporate/legal form;
- create customer authority or customer consent;
- amend Arvectum OS governance, RFCs, ADRs, Product Contracts or capability lifecycle;
- create a Position, Assignment, access grant, budget, payment authorization or technical permission;
- establish numeric financial, risk, SLA, data-volume or commitment thresholds.

Applicable law, valid corporate authority, customer rights and other higher-authority sources remain controlling within their scope.
