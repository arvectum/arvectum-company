## 4. Current action — AC-203

### AC-203 — Delegated Position authority, approval and escalation model

Status: `Current`.

Objective: define how bounded Company Organizational Authority may be delegated to accountable Positions so routine and reversible decisions can move away from the Owner without crossing the approved `ROD-*` boundary, legal/corporate competence, customer authority, Product/OS governance or approved risk/data/commitment limits.

AC-203 must determine, proportionately to current evidence:

- the minimum delegation envelope required before a Position may decide, approve or execute a bounded class of work;
- Position authority scope independent of the current human/AI/software executor;
- decision/action classes for routine delegated decisions, bounded automatic execution, approval-required consequential actions and mandatory escalation;
- financial/budget, external-commitment, data/classification, risk/consequence and duration/review limits where evidence supports them;
- excluded decisions, with AC-202 `ROD-01` through `ROD-09` as a hard negative boundary unless AC-202 is explicitly amended;
- approval semantics and evidence requirements;
- escalation/fail-closed behavior for ambiguous authority, stale facts, threshold exceedance or material exceptions;
- emergency containment boundaries that do not delegate material risk acceptance;
- the minimum canonical delegation record/reference needed for AC-204 through AC-206.

AC-203 must remain executor-neutral. It must not assign named humans/AI/software, create credentials, invent legal powers, customer authority or Product/Arvectum OS governance.

The bounded AC-108 discovery loop continues in parallel as previously authorized P1 market-evidence work.

The next Phase 2 handoff after AC-203 is `AC-204 — Initial Position Registry`.
