## 2. Approved Initial Position Registry

AC-204 `1.0.0` establishes six durable Company Positions:

| ID | Position | Primary AC-201 coverage | Initial lifecycle state |
|---|---|---|---|
| `POS-001` | Company Executive | `F-01 + F-07` | `Defined — Unassigned` |
| `POS-002` | Commercial & Customer Lead | `F-02 + F-03` | `Defined — Unassigned` |
| `POS-003` | Portfolio & Product Lead | `F-04` | `Defined — Unassigned` |
| `POS-004` | Engineering & Release Lead | `F-05` | `Defined — Unassigned` |
| `POS-005` | Finance & Obligation Control Lead | `F-06` | `Defined — Unassigned` |
| `POS-006` | Security, Risk & Continuity Lead | `F-08` | `Defined — Unassigned` |

The separation between `POS-005` and `POS-006` is part of the approved organizational accountability model. It does not imply separate current employees: AC-205 may assign the same Principal to multiple Positions while preserving distinct Position capacities, evidence and authority boundaries.

## 3. Authority boundary

The Initial Position Registry creates durable accountability units, not independent sources of Organizational Authority.

Owner, participant/general-meeting competence and General Director legal office remain separate authority capacities and are not created as Positions.

Approved AC-202 `ROD-01` through `ROD-09` remain reserved final Owner decisions. Approved AC-203 remains the governing delegation model.

The initial Position design ceiling is `AM-0`, `AM-1` and `AM-2` only, by the exact decision/action classes and exclusions in the incorporated proposal. No Position receives `AM-3` or `AM-4` by this publication.

Actual executable authority additionally requires a valid AC-205 Assignment, applicable AC-206 technical/data/tool access and current workflow/data/risk conditions.

## 4. Executor-neutral rule

Six Positions do not mean six employees or six AI agents.

A Position may later be realized by a human, AI, software or hybrid Assignment; one Principal may hold several Position Assignments; external services may provide inputs without becoming Company Positions; and executor replacement does not alter Position meaning by implication.

Assignments remain AC-205. Access remains AC-206. Continuity/replacement proof remains AC-207.

## 5. Company / Product / Arvectum OS / customer boundary

The Position Registry governs Company organizational accountability only.

It does not create customer authority, transfer product implementation truth into the Company repository, or create Arvectum OS governance authority, Product Contract or capability lifecycle state.
