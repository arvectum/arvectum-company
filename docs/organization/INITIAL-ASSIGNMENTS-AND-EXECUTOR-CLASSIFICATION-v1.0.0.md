## 3. Authority and access boundary

Assignments do not create authority beyond the approved Position ceiling and do not imply technical access.

The effective action boundary remains:

```text
Position authority envelope
∩ Assignment scope
∩ technical authorization/access
∩ current workflow/data/risk conditions
= maximum executable action
```

Approved AC-202 `ROD-01` through `ROD-09` remain reserved Owner final decisions. No `AM-3` or `AM-4` is activated by AC-205.

Owner, participant/general-meeting competence, General Director legal capacity and internal Position Assignment remain distinct even where the same natural person currently carries several capacities.

## 4. External-service and future-human boundary

The outsourced accounting/tax provider remains an external professional interface and source of accounting/statutory inputs in its valid contour. It does not become a Company Position or source of Company Organizational Authority.

Future live sellers under `POS-002` are a conditional Assignment class only. AC-205 does not create employment, a current Principal, or a separate Sales Position. A seller becomes an active Assignment only through an explicit later record after actual engagement.

## 5. AI boundary

AI/software are replaceable execution means, not independent authority sources.

In hybrid Positions, AI advises/prepares/analyses while attributable human/Owner decisions remain distinct.

`POS-004` is intentionally AI-led for bounded engineering execution. AI may perform admitted `AM-0`/`AM-1`/`AM-2` technical work without per-task Owner approval, but must stop/escalate when scope, customer acceptance, material risk/data/security, consequential external effects, missing access or `ROD-*` boundaries are implicated.

A specific model, coding agent, runtime or vendor is not the Position and may be replaced without changing Position meaning.

## 6. Downstream handoff

AC-206 must now define the actual Company data/tool/credential access boundaries required by these Assignments using least privilege and preserving the distinction between technical access and Organizational Authority.

AC-207 must later test continuity, replacement and manual/degraded fallback, including replacement of AI runtimes, future seller handoff, accounting-provider replacement and Owner unavailability without inferred reserved authority.
