## 2. Approved disposition

The approved direction is:

**`CONTINUE WITH CHANGE — bounded evidence phase`.**

The Company continues `WF-M5-001` because existing real evidence supports meaningful control/reconstructability value, while the next investment remains intentionally bounded and focused on stronger empirical evidence rather than infrastructure expansion.

## 3. Binding business/economic interpretation

Current evidence supports control value, including:

- prevention of unsupported engineering admission on the real CL-3/W11 case;
- attributable human Organizational Authority at the POS-002 gate;
- no false customer acceptance from silence or technical state;
- reconstructable bounded case state;
- provenance-preserving W11 recovery.

Current evidence does **not** support claims of profitability, revenue/margin uplift, lower total delivery cost, improved customer satisfaction or cross-product platform economics.

Unknown cost/value remains unknown rather than being treated as zero.

## 4. Approved bounded evidence phase

The next qualifying real cases should capture, where practical and without introducing heavy bureaucracy:

- Owner intervention count and minutes;
- coarse engineering effort avoided/incurred;
- cycle/rework evidence;
- tool/runtime cost when actually available;
- explicit customer validation/rework/acceptance evidence where reached;
- evidence about whether W1/W2 handling can be simplified safely.

The Company should prefer evidence gained from real work over creating new workflow infrastructure merely to complete M5.

## 5. Preserved constraints

The approved direction preserves:

- CL-3/W11 fail-closed behavior;
- current POS-002/POS-004 authority boundaries;
- AM-3/AM-4 inactive;
- customer acceptance as explicit external evidence;
- no automatic send/deploy/commitment/acceptance;
- existing data/access boundaries;
- AC-503 `NO-ADDITIONAL-OS-RELIANCE` for the first M5 proof;
- no cross-product generalization/platformization without stronger evidence.
